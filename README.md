# Jacob Moody

Independent researcher. I build behavioral health AI systems and study the conditions that make mental health crises **predictable, measurable, and often preventable** before anyone says anything.

---

## The core finding

In a 66-agent behavioral simulation, **47.2% of individuals at their highest point of risk produced zero verbal indicators of distress.** Fisher's exact p = 0.0005. Cliff's d = 1.000.

The mental health system was built around disclosure. The data says nearly half of the people who need help most don't say a word.

I built HALO to close that gap.

## HALO Systems

A longitudinal behavioral health AI platform. Built solo, no funding, two years of continuous development starting from a house fire on January 31, 2025 — the same day as my first therapy intake, PHQ-9 of 21.

First 45-day deployment: **PHQ-9 7. GAD-7 8.** Clinician-documented.

**Two lanes:**
- **Caregiver app** — live on Render, real users, behavioral logging + AI that adapts to what the trend data shows
- **Research platform** — local, agentic, wired to public health surveillance datasets (NSDUH, BRFSS, CDC WONDER)

## Theoretical frameworks

Both pre-registered at the Open Science Framework. Both have timestamps that predate the analysis.

**Cascade Compression Theory** — technological and geopolitical acceleration compresses co-regulatory social structures. When compression outpaces adaptation, behavioral health crises scale predictably. Measurably. Before the headlines.

**The Sentinel Hypothesis** — two behavioral phenotypes reaching the same crisis endpoint by opposite trajectories. One visible. One silent. The silent one is the detection gap.
